# The 31415926 r=16 row clears its floor at N=7, and it had no references at all

**Measured 2026-08-18, branch `floors-n-campaign`, on the Threadripper.**

`docs/PARITY.md` §2:

```
| overworld | 31415926 | r=16, 1,089 chunks | 107,003,486 | 39,143 | 49,570 (N=2) 🚫 UNDER FLOOR |
```

Two things were wrong with that verdict and only one of them was the N.

## There were no references

`docs/DETERMINISM.md` §4.5 priced an r=16 ladder for this seed at "0 references
on the box, 8 x 70 s ≈ 10 min, re-takeable" on 2026-08-10 and nobody took it.
Eight days later there were still none. **The 49,570 floor belonged to a
reference pair that is not on this machine and cannot be re-derived from this
tree** — the same condition as the `pair = "destroyed"` entries, but reached by
never having been saved rather than by the disk failure.

So the row was not a weak measurement. It was a verdict quoted against a number
nothing on the box could reproduce.

Eight `rows` references now exist (`floors-ow31415926r16-{a..h}`, ports
26101-26108, plus `rr-a`/`rr-b` at `rows_rev`), harvested serially at 57-183 s
each. Their pairwise width is **34,179 .. 54,175, median 45,842** over 28 pairs
— the published 49,570 sits inside that range, so it was a legitimate reading
of a single pair. It was never wrong. It was one draw from a distribution 20,000
blocks wide, used to adjudicate.

## The verdict, at every N

Our dump generated `RUSTMC_DECORATE_ORDER=zx` to match the references' own
`dump_order=zx` stamp, before any level was taken. Vanilla first in every diff.

| 1 | 34,179..54,175 | 54,175 | 36,779..48,784 | under floor |

| 4 | 85,772..97,885 | 21,004 | 12,552..17,710 | under floor |

| 6 | 106,788..109,458 | 12,028 | 12,021..13,278 | **straddles** |
| 7 | 113,938 | **8,859** | **11,861..12,542** | **CLEARS** |

**11,861..12,542 of 107,053,056 — 99.988284% to 99.988920% — against an
innocent-vanilla floor of 4,758..8,859 at the same N.** Read against the
maximum innocent charge the separation is 1.34x; against the median 6,243 it is
1.90x.

## Why this row moved and the other two 🚫 rows will not

The residual falls slowly and the floor falls fast:

```
             N=5      N=6      N=7     per reference
residual  12,244   12,021   11,861      -1.6%
floor     15,442   12,028    8,859     -24.3%
```

That is the signature of a **real defect count sitting on ground vanilla
reproduces**. A residual that were mostly noise would fall at the floor's rate,
because it would be made of the same positions.

It is also why the row could only be rescued by changing instrument, not by
buying references for the old one. The published 39,143 is a **twin-masked**
residual and its null is `union_N`, which goes 49,570 -> 113,938 as references
are added. On that instrument this row gets *worse* with N, for ever. The
measurement above is the admissible instrument, whose null is `innocent_N`, and
the two are different measurements rather than two fundings of one.

## Carry these caveats with it

* **The floor has not converged.** It is still falling 24% per reference at
  N=7, so 8,859 is an upper bound and the separation should widen. The verdict
  is established in direction; the number is not settled and should be re-taken
  at N=15.
* **1.34x is weak.** Seed 12345's admissible row clears by 14x at N=5. This one
  clears by less than half an order of magnitude on the conservative reading,
  and one unlucky candidate in a wider pool could pull the max back over it —
  a max is an extreme-value statistic and a bigger pool gets more chances to
  draw badly (`ci/floor-vs-n.py`'s own warning).
* **It is not comparable to the 39,143 it replaces.** Different instrument,
  different denominator (107,053,056 unmasked against 107,003,486 masked). The
  old row is retired by being labelled, not overwritten.
